[ 4.A ]

Basic Surface Attack (BSA)

START HERE

BSA is the entry point to the entire air-to-ground mission set — before smart weapons, before SEAD, before CAS. It's the geometry and discipline of putting an unguided weapon on a target: the attack pattern, dive angle, release parameters, and a clean recovery. Learn it the way the real pipeline teaches it.
