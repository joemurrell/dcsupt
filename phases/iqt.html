PHASE 1 // INITIAL QUALIFICATION TRAINING

IQT

Initial Qualification Training is where every pilot starts. Before you ever drop a weapon or pull a hard turn, you need to know how to fly the jet — start it, taxi it, take off, fly formation, navigate, and bring it home. Real squadrons gate combat training on IQT completion. So do we.

[ OBJ ]

Phase Objectives

WHAT YOU'LL LEARN

Aircraft Knowledge

Systems, limits, memory items, and emergency procedures. Know your jet before you fly it.

Basic Airmanship

Ground ops, takeoff, traffic pattern, instrument flying, formation, and landing.

Flight Discipline

Comms, checklist usage, situational awareness, and operating as a wingman.
