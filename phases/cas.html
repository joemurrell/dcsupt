PHASE 5 // CLOSE AIR SUPPORT

Close Air Support

The hardest job in the air-to-ground mission set. Coordinating with ground forces, executing 9-lines, working with JTACs, deconflicting with friendlies, and making split-second decisions with lives on the line.

[ 5.A ]

CAS Fundamentals & Procedures

THE 12-STEP FLOW

CAS is where everything comes together — your surface-attack fundamentals from the Strike phase, your comms discipline, and tight coordination with a JTAC. It's the most demanding air-to-ground job because friendlies are danger close. Start with the flow, learn the 9-line, then study the control types.

REF-C01
CAS Flow for Dummies — The 12 Steps of CAS
Walks the standard CAS flow end to end. The "12 steps" framework is the backbone of every CAS engagement — check-in, game plan, 9-line, read-back, talk-on, clearance, attack. Start here.
