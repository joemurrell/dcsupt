CARRIER MULTIROLE STRIKE FIGHTER

F/A-18C Hornet

The benchmark multirole fighter for DCS — and arguably the best entry point into the modern fighter sim. Air-to-air, strike, SEAD, CAS, and carrier operations all in one airframe, with a deep but learnable systems suite.

[ SPEC ]

Quick Reference

AIRFRAME DATA

Role

Carrier-capable multirole strike fighter. Equally at home in A/A and A/G missions.

Difficulty

Approachable systems with deep mastery curve. One of the friendlier "complex" modules.

Best For

First serious module. Pilots who want to learn one jet that does everything well.
