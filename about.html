About

DCS UPT is a curated training pipeline for new pilots in DCS World — built on the structure of real USAF undergraduate pilot training, populated with the best learning resources the sim community has produced.

[ 01 ]

Why This Exists

DCS has a learning cliff, not a curve. The official tutorials are uneven, the YouTube content is scattered across hundreds of channels, and new pilots often don't know what they don't know — let alone what order to learn things in.

Real squadrons solve this with a structured training pipeline: IQT, MQT, then mission qualifications. The progression isn't arbitrary — each phase builds the foundation for the next. This site borrows that structure and uses it to organize the best free training content in the community.

// MISSION STATEMENT

Help new DCS pilots reach competency faster by giving them a clear path, proven resources, and the structure to know what to learn next.

// NOT-MISSION

This is not a replacement for flying with a squadron. The best learning happens in the air with people who'll tell you when you screwed up. Use this to prepare for that.
